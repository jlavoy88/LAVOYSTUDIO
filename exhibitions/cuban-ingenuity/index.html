Curatorial project · 2019

Cuban Ingenuity

The Artistry of the Everyday Inventor. Mid-century American cars, Soviet washing machines and household objects, repaired, rebuilt and reinvented across three generations of scarcity in Cuba — shown as works of invention. Co-curated by Gabriela Azcuy and Jorge Lavoy.

VenueCade Museum for Creativity and Invention
CityGainesville, Florida
DatesMarch 10 – December 31, 2019
CuratorsGabriela Azcuy and Jorge Lavoy
WithAnne Gilroy, Thomas Center Galleries
The exhibition

Invention born of scarcity

Some 45 to 50 objects, photographs and videos gathered on trips across Cuba: a Soviet washing-machine motor turned into a lawnmower, improvised electric water heaters, cars kept running decades past their parts supply. Each shows a solution built from whatever was at hand, especially after the economic collapse of the 1990s.

The exhibition was the centerpiece of Bulla Cubana 2019, a Gainesville-wide program of Cuban art and culture. Lavoy's production company, Next Art Project, also made the short film Cuban Ingenuity for the museum.

“It’s kind of a mindset. You just grow up with that.”

— Jorge Lavoy, Radio Cade, March 2019
