Solo exhibition · 2017

Empty Space

Wall works and objects in pigmented resin, wood, glass and aluminum: fragments of color suspended in frames, scattered across walls and set against bare ground. Lavoy described each piece as a calibration exercise — a frequency from the body, made visible.

VenueKendall Art Center (now MoCAA)
CityMiami, Florida
OpeningMay 26, 2017
CuratorGabriela García Azcuy
Works13 works and series
MediaPigmented resin, wood, glass, aluminum, oil
Exhibition text

Calibration exercises

“Nothing exists except atoms and empty space; everything else is opinion.”

— Democritus

English

All the pieces in Empty Space are frequencies emitted by my body, dictated by the subconscious mind. These are calibration exercises. The final result is a wave — the piece — which, emitted towards dark matter, will travel the universe until it returns to me again.

Español

Todas las piezas en Empty Space son frecuencias emitidas por mi cuerpo, dictadas por la mente subconsciente. Son ejercicios de calibración. El resultado final es una onda — la pieza — la cual, emitida a la materia oscura, transitará el universo hasta regresar a mí nuevamente.

— Jorge Lavoy
