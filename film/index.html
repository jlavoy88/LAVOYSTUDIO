Next Art Project · Direction & Production

Film & Video

Since founding the audiovisual production company Next Art Project in Havana in 2013, Jorge Lavoy has filmed exhibitions, installations and artists at work — for the Kennedy Center, the Art Museum of the Americas and the Cade Museum — alongside making-of films for musicians and artists and aerial studies of the North Florida landscape.
